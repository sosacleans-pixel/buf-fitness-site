Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information buildurfitness.com collects and how it is used. The site is operated by Coach Salim Heyaime LLC, a Utah limited liability company (“BUF,” “we,” “us”). BUF (Build Ur Fitness) is a Coach Salim Heyaime LLC project. Questions? Email coachsalimheyaime@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

Waitlist form. When you join the waitlist, we collect the details you type in: your name, your email address, whether you are looking for a coach or are a coach, and any goals or questions you choose to share. Please don’t include medical or other sensitive information in the message box.

Usage data. We use Google Analytics (Google Analytics 4) to understand how people use the site: pages viewed, how you arrived, approximate location (city or region), device, browser and similar technical details. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to do this. We also record anonymous events when you click a link to Amazon or send the waitlist form (which link, or that the form was sent); these never include your name, email or message.

Server and font requests. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services receive your IP address and basic browser information when your device requests a page or a font.

We don’t ask for payment details, and the site has no user accounts.

2. How we use it

  • To contact you about BUF: launch news, early access and replies to your questions.
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve the site.
  • To keep the form free of spam and abuse.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t share it for other companies’ advertising.

3. Services that process your information

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers waitlist form entries to our email inbox. It may show a captcha to block spam. See FormSubmit’s privacy policy.
  • Google provides Google Analytics, Google Fonts and the Gmail inbox where form entries arrive. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • GitHub hosts the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The site still works without analytics cookies.

5. How long we keep it

We keep waitlist entries until BUF launches or until you ask us to remove you, whichever comes first, unless you become a client, in which case we keep what we need to coach you. Google Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting in our Analytics account.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information you sent through the form, or to stop emailing you. Email coachsalimheyaime@gmail.com and we’ll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

7. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we’ll delete it.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of sending or storing information online is perfectly secure, but we limit access to waitlist entries to the people who need them to run BUF.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
