How it works

Three steps. One coach in your corner.

1

Tell us your goals

A short intake covers your goals, schedule, equipment, and any injuries or limits.

2

Get a coach-approved plan

AI drafts a personalized program; your coach reviews and adjusts it before you ever see it.

3

Train with text check-ins

Daily nudges, quick questions answered, plans adapted — with your coach watching the thread.

Human-in-the-loop

Not a faceless bot. A coach who knows you.

AI handles the speed and the busywork. A real coach owns the decisions. Every plan change and every outgoing message is supervised by the coach assigned to you.

  • Coaches review, edit, or reject AI drafts
  • Sensitive topics (injury, pain, nutrition concerns) route straight to your coach
  • You always know who your coach is — by name
You text a question or check-in
