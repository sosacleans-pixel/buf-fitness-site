How it works

Three steps. One coach in your corner.

1

Tell us your goals

A short intake covers your goals, schedule, equipment, and any injuries or limits.

2

Get a coach-approved plan

We build a program around you; your coach reviews and fine-tunes it before you ever see it.

3

Train with text check-ins

Daily nudges, quick questions answered, plans adapted — with your coach watching the thread.

Human-in-the-loop

Not a faceless bot. A coach who knows you.

Behind the scenes, smart tools handle the busywork. Your coach makes the calls. Every plan change and every outgoing message is supervised by the coach assigned to you.

  • Your coach reviews, edits, or rewrites every plan and reply
  • Sensitive topics (injury, pain, nutrition concerns) route straight to your coach
  • You always know who your coach is — by name
You text a question or check-in
BUF prepares a reply or plan adjustment
Your coach approves, edits, or takes over
You get fast, personal, accountable coaching
