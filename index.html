How it works

Three steps. One coach in your corner.

1

Tell us your goals

A short intake covers your goals, schedule, equipment, and any injuries or limits.

2

Get a coach-approved plan

We build a program around you; Coach Salim reviews and fine-tunes it before you ever see it.

3

Train with text check-ins

Daily nudges, quick questions answered, plans adapted, all by text inside the BUF app, with Coach Salim watching the thread.

Human-in-the-loop

Not a faceless bot. A coach who knows you.

Behind the scenes, smart tools handle the busywork. Coach Salim makes the calls. Every plan change and every outgoing message is supervised by him.

  • Coach Salim reviews, edits, or rewrites every plan and reply
  • Sensitive topics (injury, pain, nutrition concerns) go straight to Coach Salim
  • You always know who your coach is: Coach Salim, by name
You text a question or check-in inside the app
BUF prepares a reply or plan adjustment
Coach Salim approves, edits, or takes over
You get fast, personal, accountable coaching
